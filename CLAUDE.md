# CLAUDE.md

## Judging criteria — every decision must serve these

- **Project Vision & 0G Fit**
- **Technical Approach & Architecture**
- **Team & Execution Signal**
- **Product-Market Fit** — clear potential to attract and retain users
- **Innovation and Creativity** — original approaches that push boundaries
- **Smart contract quality** — best practices, structured logically and efficiently, minimal security vulnerabilities

## Rules

- The product must not be AI slop from any angle. It must look and read as human-made, with proficiency and professionalism.
- Nothing half-baked. Anything commanded gets built fully.
- Nothing is a demo. Everything real.
- Never guess. Follow the docs.
- Work priority-wise: highest priority first, then the next, in that order, always organized.

## Sources — read before building

- `oglabs resources/` — all 0G info (SDKs, endpoints, chain IDs, patterns)
- `PRD.md` — what we're building and why
- `FEATURES.md` — the ranked feature list
- Global `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` — always applies

## Open decision

PRD says v1 needs no smart contracts, but the criteria require smart contract quality. Ask the user before choosing either path.
